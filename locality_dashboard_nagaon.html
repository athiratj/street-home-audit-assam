How to read the colours
Effort scale (audits, fellows) — light to dark blue as count increases
Concern scale (critical count, issue density, burn/dump %) — white to red as severity rises
Positive-outcome scale (coverage %, segregation %) — white to green as % rises
Diversity scale (# issue categories seen) — informational, purple as it rises
Column key: Total audits + audit-type columns = fellow effort · Density = issues per audit · Diversity = distinct issue categories seen
Coverage = % of surveyed homes with door-to-door collector · Home segregation = % who always separate wet/dry · Burn/dump = % who burn or dump when collector misses
“—” = insufficient household data in that locality to compute the metric.
Reading small-sample rows: the Total audits column is the reliability signal — a red cell on 1–2 audits is a lead to investigate, not a conclusion. Check total audits before acting on any extreme value.
